     What cannot be explained by harmonic lattice potentials

Macroscopic phenomena:
• Thermal expansion of the lattice
• Temperature dependence of elastic moduli
• Weak growth of 𝐶𝐶𝑉𝑉 above the Debye temperature
• Finite thermal conductivity (harmonic potential: infinite)

Microscopic phenomena:
• Phonon decay
• Quantum harmonic oscillator energy levels depend on 𝑛𝑛 (𝜔𝜔𝑛𝑛 ≠ 𝜔𝜔)
• Equilibrium position of harmonic oscillator depends on 𝑛𝑛 (=> explains thermal
