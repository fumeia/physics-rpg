Question 1 [Stern–Gerlach experiment ]:
In the Stern–Gerlach experiment, (electrically neutral) particles are shot through an in-
homogeneous magnetic field. A particle is deflected up- or downwards depending on its
intrinsic angular momentum, its spin. Finally, the path the particle has taken is measured
by recording its incidence on a screen.
    Sketch the expected measurement results of Stern–Gerlach experiments carried out
with molecules composed of
  (i) two spin- 12 atoms.
 (ii) three spin- 21 atoms.
