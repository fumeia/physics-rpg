Question 2 [SO(4) ]:
Consider the Lie algebra of SO(4),

            so(4) = {γ̇(t)|t=0 : γ(t) differentiable path in SO(4), γ(0) = id} .                 (7)

  (i) Describe so(4) as a space of matrices.

 (ii) Find a basis {A1 , A2 , A3 , B1 , B2 , B3 } of so(4) such that

                    [Ai , Aj ] = εijk Ak ,    [Bi , Bj ] = εijk Ak ,    [Ai , Bj ] = εijk Bk .   (8)

(iii) Show that so(4) is isomorphic, as a Lie algebra, to su(2) ⊕ su(2). (Hint: Find
      generators of so(4) such that they split into two commuting sets of su(2) generators.)
