Exercise 3: Molecular orbitals from the wave functions
of the atomic orbitals
To describe covalent bonds, we construct the molecular orbitals from linear combinations
of the atomic orbitals. In this exercise, we model the atomic orbital of each individual
atom as the bound state of a delta-function potential. To construct the Hamiltonian, we
explicitly calculate the onsite energy and the hopping from these atomic orbitals. Diago-
nalizing this Hamiltonian yields the molecular orbitals.

Consider an electron moving in 1D between two negative delta-function potential wells.
The full Hamiltonian of this one-dimensional system is:

                         Ĥ =       − V0 δ(x1 − x) − V0 δ(x2 − x),
                                2m
where V0 > 0 is the potential strength, p̂ the momentum of the electron, and x1 and x2
are the positions of the potential wells.

Let us find the molecular orbitals. As trial wave function for the molecular orbital, we
consider a linear combination of the two atomic orbitals |1⟩ and |2⟩:
