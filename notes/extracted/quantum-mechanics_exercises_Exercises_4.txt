Question 2 [Infinite Square Well ]:
   For the one-dimensional square well V = −V0 for |x| < a and V = 0 otherwise, show
that in the limit in which the depth V0 → ∞ while the width a → 0 with V0 a constant,
the ground state energy E0 remains finite (non-zero and negative).

  (i) Let V (x) be an arbitrary potential in one dimension, with V (x) → 0 as x →
      ±∞. Let χ(x) be a stationary state for a particle scattering off this potential,
      with asymptotic behaviour χ(x) = eikx + Ae−ikx for x ≪ 0 and χ(x) = Beikx for
