Question 2 [Quantum Zeno effect ]:

     where ⟨H⟩ is the expectation value of H in the state |ϕ⟩.

                           lim P Ut/n P Ut/n · · · P Ut/n P = e−i⟨H⟩t/ℏ P .            (5)

Note: This exhibits the fact that abrupt changes of states caused by measurements are
radically different from the steady evolution described by the Schrödinger equation. Here
(eq. (3)) we repeated the measurement P = |ϕ⟩⟨ϕ|, over and over again, on an evolving
system. This led to eq. (5), which shows that repeated measurements ‘freeze’ quantum
states: the system never moves away from |ϕ⟩.
