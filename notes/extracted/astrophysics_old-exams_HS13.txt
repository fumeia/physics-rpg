Ablauf ((Begrüssung))
Prof: Can you tell me about the structure of the sun?
Ich: The sun has a radiative core and a convective shell.
Prof: What does convective and radiative physically mean?
Ich: They are heat transfer processes. Convection involves displacement of matter (like the bubbles
when water boils for instance), whereas radiation is heat transport through electromagnetic waves.
Prof: Why do we have convection on the outside and radiation on the inside?
Ich: Keyword: Schwarzschild stability condition. On the inside of the sun, the fusion process (pp-chain)
isn’t efficient enough to produce a temperature gradient that is higher as Schwarzschild gradient so
the system is stable to convection. On the outside, the structure of the sun is very opaque and thus
produces a higher gradient of temperature so that the system is instable to convection.
Prof: What about stars of bigger sizes?
Ich: There it is the opposite: convective core and radiative radius.
Prof: What is the main source of energy inside the sun?
Ich: Nuclear fusion. Hydrogen into helium conversion.
Prof: What happens when we don’t have any fuel anymore? How does the star evolve?
Ich: HR-diagramm: main sequence → red giant → ends up in: white dwarf, neutron star, supernova or
blackhole depending on the mass of the star.


Prof: Good, next topic: What can you tell me about rotation curves of spiral galaxies?
Ich: They are evidence for dark matter: Rotation curves (vc ) are linear outside the visible radius
