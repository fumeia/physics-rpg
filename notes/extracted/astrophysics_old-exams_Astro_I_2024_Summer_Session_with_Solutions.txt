1. (3 points) If star S1 is observed to have a parallax angle of 0.25 arcseconds due to the
   rotation of the earth about the Sun, and star S2 is located four times further away from
   Earth than star S1, what is the approximate parallax angle of star S2?
          □ 1.00 arcseconds
          □ 0.50 arcseconds
          ■ 0.0625 arcseconds
          □ 0.125 arcseconds


    Solution: The parallax angle θ = 1 dAU , therefore if star S2 is four times further away,
    it will be observed with a four times smaller parallax angle, θ = 0.25′′ /4 = 0.0625′′ .


2. (3 points) Our solar system is situated approximately 8 kpc from the Galactic Centre.
   If one were to observe a star located 10 kpc from us in the direction of Galactic longitude
   l=180◦ and latitude b=0◦ , what is the approximate distance of this star from the Galactic
   Centre?
          □ 2 kpc
          □ 8 kpc
          □ 10 kpc
          ■ 18 kpc


    Solution: l=180◦ is in the opposite direction from the Galactic Centre and therefore
    the star is 8 kpc + 10 kpc = 18 kpc from the Galactic centre.

6. (3 points) Which of the following statements correctly describes the relationship between
   a star’s observed color and its surface temperature, Teff ?
         ■ Hotter stars have a thermal spectrum peaking at higher νmax and are therefore
           bluer.
         □ Cooler stars have a thermal spectrum peaking at lower νmax and are therefore
           bluer.
         □ Cooler stars have a thermal spectrum peaking at higher νmax and are therefore
           redder.
         □ Hotter stars have a thermal spectrum peaking at lower νmax and are therefore
           bluer.


    Solution: From Wien’s displacement law, νmax ∝ T , and bluer stars emit more at
    higher ν. Therefore hotter stars emit at higher frequencies and appear bluer.

    namical friction refers to a net gravitational force on that particle directed
           ■ backwards relative to its motion due to the enhancement of the particle density
             in its wake
           □ forwards relative to its motion due to the enhancement of the particle density

11. (3 points) Synchrotron radiation is observed in astrophysics and is produced by
           □ neutrinos changing flavours as they travel from the sun
           □ particles self gravitating in a stellar system
           □ dark matter particles during virialization

     in supernova remnants, radio galaxies, quasars and the interstellar medium.

14. (3 points) If the sum of the angles of any small triangle on a 2-dimensional surface is
    greater than 180◦ , the surface has
          □ curvature equal to -1
          □ negative curvature
          □ zero curvature
          ■ positive curvature


     Solution: The sum of the angles of (small) triangles on a 2-dimensional flat surface
     (a plane) is equal to 180◦ . If the sum of the angles of small triangles is greater than
     180◦ , the surface has positive curvature, as is the case for a 2-dimensional sphere for
     example.

    temperature of the universe corresponded to energy scale of
          □ about 1 GeV
          □ about 1 MeV
          ■ about 1 eV
          □ about 0.01 eV


     Solution: During recombination, nuclei and electrons combine to form atoms. This
     occurred at an energy scale of about 1 eV, which is slightly smaller than the binding
     energy of the hydrogen atom (∼ 13.6 eV) due to the large number of photons.
