## Conventions
- The script's generators are dimensionless: $M_i=i\Omega_i$ (7.24), $[M_1,M_2]=iM_3$ (7.25); the sheets and exams use $L_i$ with $\hbar$: $[L_j,L_k]=i\hbar\varepsilon_{jkl}L_l$. $M_\pm=M_1\pm iM_2$, $[M_3,M_\pm]=\pm M_\pm$, $[M_+,M_-]=2M_3$.
- $L_j=\varepsilon_{jkl}x_kp_l=-i\hbar\varepsilon_{jkl}x_k\partial_l$; $\vec L=\vec r\wedge\vec p$. Rotation of wave functions $(U(R)\psi)(x)=\psi(R^{-1}x)$ (7.5); in HS25 Q1 $(U(R)\psi)(x)=\psi(R^Tx)$ and $L\psi=i\hbar\frac{d}{d\alpha}U(R(\alpha))\psi|_0=-i\hbar\partial_\varphi\psi$.
- Sign convention of the exam hints: $L_\pm|j,m\rangle=\hbar\sqrt{j(j+1)-m(m\pm1)}\,|j,m\pm1\rangle$.

## 1. so(3) and its representations (script 7.2-7.4)
- $so(3)$ = real antisymmetric $3\times3$ matrices, $\Omega(\vec\omega)\vec x=\vec\omega\wedge\vec x$, $[\Omega(\vec\omega_1),\Omega(\vec\omega_2)]=\Omega(\vec\omega_1\wedge\vec\omega_2)$; $e^{\Omega(\vec\omega)t}$ is the rotation about $\vec\omega$ by $|\vec\omega|t$. Unitary representations: $U(\Omega)^\dagger=-U(\Omega)$, so $M=iU(\Omega)$ is Hermitian.
- Derivation of $[L_i,L_j]=i\hbar\varepsilon_{ijk}L_k$ (sheet 8 Q1(i), FS26 Q1(i), FS22 Q2(i)): $[L_1,L_2]=[x_2p_3-x_3p_2,\,x_3p_1-x_1p_3]=x_2[p_3,x_3]p_1+p_2[x_3,p_3]x_1=i\hbar(x_1p_2-x_2p_1)=i\hbar L_3$; the rest by cyclicity. $[L_3,\vec L^2]=0$ because $[L_3,L_1^2+L_2^2]=i\hbar(L_2L_1+L_1L_2-L_1L_2-L_2L_1)=0$.
- Irreps (7.37)-(7.51): highest weight $M_+\psi_j=0$, $M_3\psi_j=j\psi_j$; $M_+\psi_m=\mu_m\psi_{m+1}$ with $\mu_m=j(j+1)-m(m+1)$; the lowering chain must stop, $\mu_{j-k-1}=0\Rightarrow2j=k$, so $j\in\{0,\frac12,1,\frac32,\dots\}$, $\dim D^j=2j+1$. Casimir $\vec M^2=M_-M_++M_3(M_3+1)=M_+M_-+M_3(M_3-1)$, value $j(j+1)$ ($j(j+1)=(j-k)(j-k-1)$ again gives $k=2j$). 
- $D^1$: $L_3=\hbar\,\mathrm{diag}(1,0,-1)$, $L_\pm=\hbar\sqrt2(\ldots)$, $L_1=\frac\hbar{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}$, $L_2=\frac\hbar{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}0&-i&0\\i&0&-i\\0&i&0\end{pmatrix}$ in the basis $|1,1\rangle,|1,0\rangle,|1,-1\rangle$. The defining and the adjoint rep of $so(3)$ are $D^1$.
- Spherical harmonics $Y_{\ell m}$ carry $D^\ell$ ($\ell$ integer): $M_3=-i\partial_\varphi$, $M_\pm=e^{\pm i\varphi}(\pm\partial_\theta+i\cot\theta\,\partial_\varphi)$; $Y_{\ell\ell}\propto\sin^\ell\theta\,e^{i\ell\varphi}$ (killed by $M_+$).
- Expectation values in $|l,m\rangle$ (sheet 8 Q1(ii), FS22 Q2(iii)-(v)): $\langle L_1\rangle=\langle L_2\rangle=0$ ($L_\pm$ change $m$). $[L_3,L_1L_2+L_2L_1]=2i\hbar(L_2^2-L_1^2)$ and $\langle[A,B]\rangle=0$ in an eigenstate of $A$ imply $\langle L_1^2\rangle=\langle L_2^2\rangle=\frac12\hbar^2[l(l+1)-m^2]$. Then $\Delta L_1\Delta L_2=\frac12\hbar^2[l(l+1)-m^2]\ge\frac\hbar2|\langle L_3\rangle|=\frac{\hbar^2|m|}2$ since $l(l+1)-m^2\ge l\ge|m|$; equality for $|m|=l$.

## 2. SU(2) vs SO(3), spinors (script 7.5-7.7; sheet 9 Q3; sheet 11 Q2)
- $\sigma_j\sigma_k=\delta_{jk}+i\varepsilon_{jkl}\sigma_l$, $(\vec\sigma\cdot\vec a)(\vec\sigma\cdot\vec b)=\vec a\cdot\vec b+i\vec\sigma\cdot(\vec a\wedge\vec b)$. $U(\omega\vec n)=e^{-i\omega\vec n\cdot\vec\sigma/2}=\cos\frac\omega2-i\sin\frac\omega2\,\vec n\cdot\vec\sigma$ (since $(\vec n\cdot\vec\sigma)^2=1$).
- $\tilde x=\vec x\cdot\vec\sigma$, $\det\tilde x=-\vec x^2$; $\tilde x\to U\tilde xU^\dagger$ preserves length and orientation: $SU(2)\to SO(3)$, kernel $\{\pm1\}$, $SU(2)/\{\pm1\}\cong SO(3)$. Rotation by $2\pi$: $e^{-2\pi iM_3}|j,m\rangle=e^{-2\pi im}|j,m\rangle=(-1)^{2j}$: trivial only for integer $j$. Half-integer $j$ are projective representations of $SO(3)$ = true representations of the covering group $SU(2)$, all equally relevant (7.6). Electron: $\mathcal H=L^2(\mathbb R^3)\otimes\mathbb C^2$, $M_j=\sigma_j/2$.
- Sheet 11 Q2: $\langle\vec\sigma\rangle=\vec e$ (unit vector, polar angles $\theta,\varphi$) $\Rightarrow|\chi\rangle=(\cos\frac\theta2,\ e^{i\varphi}\sin\frac\theta2)^T$ (the eigenvector of $\vec e\cdot\vec\sigma$ with eigenvalue $+1$); $P(S_z=+\hbar/2)=\cos^2\frac\theta2=\frac{1+e_3}2$.
- Sheet 10 Q2 (projective representations): $G=\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2$, $\gamma(g)=\sigma_3$, $\gamma(h)=\sigma_1$, $\gamma(gh)=-i\sigma_2$ is a projective rep with cocycle $\pm1$ that cannot be removed by rephasing; covering group $E=\mathbb Z_2\times G\cong D_4$ with $(x,a)(y,b)=(xyc_2(a,b),ab)$ and $\hat\gamma(x,a)=x\gamma(a)$ is a true representation. General: $E=U\times G$, $\hat\rho(u,g)=u\rho(g)$. (Analogue of $SO(3)\leftarrow SU(2)$.)
- FS24 Q3(b): rotate the spin and measure along $z$ $\equiv$ measure the unrotated spin along $O(\vec n,-\theta)\vec e_z$ because $\langle\psi|U^\dagger\Pi_zU|\psi\rangle=\langle\psi|\Pi_{U^\dagger e_z}|\psi\rangle$.

## 3. Vector and tensor operators (sheet 12 Q2)
- $\hbar^{-1}[L_j,\cdot]$ is the action of the rotation generators on operators. $\vec J$ is a vector operator iff $[L_i,J_j]=i\hbar\varepsilon_{ijk}J_k$ (D$^1$); e.g. $[L_i,x_j]=i\hbar\varepsilon_{ijk}x_k$, same for $\vec p$, $\vec L$ and the Pauli-Lenz vector. Spherical basis: $J_{+1}=-\frac1{\sqrt2}(J_1+iJ_2)$, $J_0=J_3$, $J_{-1}=\frac1{\sqrt2}(J_1-iJ_2)$.
- $S^2D^1\cong D^2\oplus D^0$ ($6=5+1$): from two vectors $\vec J,\vec K$: scalar $S=-\frac1{\sqrt3}\vec J\cdot\vec K$ and the symmetric traceless rank-2 tensor $T_{ij}=\frac12(J_iK_j+J_jK_i)-\frac13\delta_{ij}\vec J\cdot\vec K$ (the five components $T_{\pm2}=J_{\pm1}K_{\pm1}$, $T_{\pm1}=\frac1{\sqrt2}(J_{\pm1}K_0+J_0K_{\pm1})$, $T_0=\frac1{\sqrt6}(J_{+1}K_{-1}+2J_0K_0+J_{-1}K_{+1})$). The full product $D^1\otimes D^1=D^0\oplus D^1\oplus D^2$ also contains the antisymmetric part $\vec J\wedge\vec K$ ($D^1$).
- Expectation values $\langle\phi|\vec J|\phi\rangle$ rotate like a vector.

## 4. so(4) = su(2) + su(2) (sheet 8 Q2)
$so(4)$ = real antisymmetric $4\times4$ matrices (dim 6). Basis $A_i$ (rotations of the first three coordinates), $B_i$ (rotations mixing coordinate $i$ with the 4th): $[A_i,A_j]=\varepsilon_{ijk}A_k$, $[B_i,B_j]=\varepsilon_{ijk}A_k$, $[A_i,B_j]=\varepsilon_{ijk}B_k$. $X_i=\frac12(A_i+B_i)$, $Y_i=\frac12(A_i-B_i)$ satisfy $[X_i,X_j]=\varepsilon_{ijk}X_k$, $[Y_i,Y_j]=\varepsilon_{ijk}Y_k$, $[X_i,Y_j]=0$. Used for hydrogen with $\vec S=\frac12(\vec L+a\vec J)$, $\vec D=\frac12(\vec L-a\vec J)$.

## 5. Exams
- FS26 Q1 (20 pt): $j=1$; $L_1$ measured with result $\hbar$ $\Rightarrow|\psi\rangle=\frac12(|1,1\rangle+\sqrt2|1,0\rangle+|1,-1\rangle)$ (eigenvector of $L_1=\frac\hbar{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}$); $P(L_3=\hbar,0,-\hbar)=\frac14,\frac12,\frac14$, $\langle L_3\rangle=0$; $\langle\psi|[L_1,O]|\psi\rangle=0$ since $\psi$ is an eigenvector of $L_1$ $\Rightarrow\langle L_2\rangle=0$ and $\langle L_2^2\rangle=\langle L_3^2\rangle=\hbar^2/2$ (as $L_1^2+L_2^2+L_3^2=2\hbar^2$, $\langle L_1^2\rangle=\hbar^2$); $\Delta L_1=0$, $\Delta L_2=\Delta L_3=\hbar/\sqrt2$; $\Delta L_2\Delta L_3=\hbar^2/2=\frac\hbar2|\langle L_1\rangle|$: the Heisenberg bound is saturated, the others are $0\ge0$.
- FS26 Q3 (19 pt): $[H,L_j]\psi=i\hbar\varepsilon_{jkl}x_k(\partial_lV)\psi$ (the kinetic term commutes because $[\nabla^2,x_k\partial_l]=2\partial_k\partial_l$ contracts to zero with $\varepsilon$), zero iff $\vec x\wedge\nabla V=0$ iff $V=V(r)$. Consequence: eigenstates $R(r)Y_{lm}$, $(2l+1)$-fold degeneracy. For $V=\frac12m\omega^2x^2$: $\psi_i=x_ie^{-m\omega x^2/2\hbar}$ have $E=\frac52\hbar\omega$; $L_j\psi_k=-i\hbar\varepsilon_{jak}\psi_a$ (the $\partial g\propto x_b$ part drops by antisymmetry), so $L_3\psi_1=i\hbar\psi_2$, $L_3\psi_2=-i\hbar\psi_1$, $L_3\psi_3=0$: eigenvectors $\psi_1\pm i\psi_2$ ($\pm\hbar$) and $\psi_3$ ($0$): the $l=1$ triplet.
- FS22 Q2 (15 pt): see §1. FS22 Q3(iii): $H=\vec p^2/2m+k_1x+k_2y$: $[H,p_x]=ik_1\hbar$, $[H,p_y]=ik_2\hbar$, $[H,p_z]=0$: only $\langle p_z\rangle$ conserved ($\vec L\cdot\vec k$ also conserved).
- FS19 Q3 (18 pt): $|1,1\rangle$: $\langle J_z\rangle=1$, $\langle J_x\rangle=\langle J_y\rangle=0$; $|1,0\rangle$: all zero. $D^1\otimes D^1=D^2\oplus D^1\oplus D^0$ by counting $J_z$ eigenvalues; singlet $\frac1{\sqrt3}(|1,-1\rangle-|0,0\rangle+|-1,1\rangle)$ (with the standard phase convention; the sign of the middle term depends on the convention for the lowering operators: the phase used here is $|0,0\rangle\propto|1\rangle|-1\rangle-|0\rangle|0\rangle+|-1\rangle|1\rangle$); Alice's $m=+1$ has probability $1/3$ (reduced state maximally mixed); after Alice's $m=+1$ Bob finds $m=-1$ with probability 1.
