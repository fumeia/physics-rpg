  (i) Find the normalised eigenfunctions of the Hamilton operator and the associated

 (ii) Suppose that at time t = 0 the system is described by the wave function

              2A      2πx   πy  2B      πx   2πy  2C      πx   πy 

      outcomes of the energy measurement, and what are the associated probabilities?
      In each case describe the wave function immediately after the energy has been
      measured. [4 pt]

     and y,
                                  R Ψ(x, y) = Ψ(y, x) .                        (6)

     the wave function immediately after R has been measured. [5 pt]
