 c) The heat capacity is given by (1/V )(dE/dT ), with E given in the answer to the
    previous subquestion. The calculation is similar to that for Exercise 2.
    A harmonic oscillator starts to go mostly into its ground state when the temperature
    is reduced below its Einstein temperature (note that the Einstein temperature is
    the eigenfrequency of the oscillator expressed in units of Kelvin). Therefore, upon
    reducing temperature, first the atoms with the higher eigenfrequency (or equiva-
    lently, higher Einstein temperature) will go into their ground state, and then the
    atoms with the lower eigenfrequency. Therefore, taking the lower of the two Ein-
    stein temperatures, since 7 Li is heavier than 6 Li (i.e., m2 > m1 ), we have ω2 < ω1
    and the temperature at which almost all atoms are in their ground state is given by
    ℏω2 /kB .
