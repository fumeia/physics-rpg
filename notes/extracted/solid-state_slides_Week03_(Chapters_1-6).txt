                  Metallic vs. Non-metallic chemical elements

Non-metals                                      Metals
Tend to form volatile molecules                 Crystalline solids (except Hg)
Ready to share electrons                        Do not readily share electrons, monoatomic vapors
Low density                                     High density
Electronegative, form anions (Cl-, O2-, etc.)   Electropositive, form cations (Na+, Cu2+, etc.)
Form oxides that are basic (e.g., CaO)          Form oxides that are acidic (e.g., SO2)
Poor conductors of heat and electricity         Good electrical conductors and heat conductors..
Brittle - if a solid.                           Malleable - can be beaten into thin sheets.
Nonductile.                                     Ductile - can be stretched into wire.
Do not possess metallic luster.                 Possess metallic luster.
Transparent as a thin sheet.                    Opaque as thin sheet.
