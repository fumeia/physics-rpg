 c) For the transition under consideration, both an energy change and a change in the
    wave vector are necessary. This is called an indirect band transition.
    From the values calculated above we see that the electron energy exceeds that of
    the phonon by two orders of magnitude. It is therefore hardly changed after the
    scattering process and is therefore not sufficient to bring the electron into the higher
    band.
    The wave vector of the electron, on the other hand, can be strongly influenced by
    the interaction with the phonon and thus achieve the required change.
    In order to realize an excitation in the next higher band, the energy can be intro-
    duced, for example, in the form of photons.
