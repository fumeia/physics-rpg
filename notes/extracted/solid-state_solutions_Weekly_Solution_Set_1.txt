Solution 1: Rotational symmetry
If we follow the instructions, we get a trapezoid with AB = AD = BC = a, which results
from the rotation around A and B, respectively.




Since C and D are also lattice points, the distance DC = (1 − 2 cos ϕ)a must be an integer
multiple of a − the period of the lattice. This must be true as the vector DC is in the
same direction as the vector AB. This is true only for cos ϕ = ±1, ±0.5, and 0, or in
other words, for ϕ = 0◦ , 180◦ , 120◦ , 90◦ , and 60◦ . This corresponds to rotation axes with
n = 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6.
