# Astro 04: Friedmann, early universe, black-hole growth (Choudhuri 10, 11, 9.4)
Sources: HS25 winter Q6, Q7, MC 13-15; HS24 summer Q7, MC 14, 15.

- Flat Friedmann $(\dot a/a)^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho$ with $\rho\propto a^{-3(1+w)}$: $a\propto t^{\alpha}$, $\alpha=\frac{2}{3(1+w)}$ (matter $2/3$, radiation $1/2$). $H=\alpha/t$, age $t_0=\alpha/H_0$: $9.3$ Gyr for matter only at $H_0=70$.
- Radiation era: $T\propto a^{-1}\propto t^{-1/2}$. $T(1\,\mathrm{s})=1.5\times10^{10}$ K gives $t_{BBN}(10^9\,\mathrm{K})=225$ s.
- $1+z=\lambda_{obs}/\lambda_{em}=\nu_{em}/\nu_{obs}$; $T\propto1+z$. 21 cm at 700 MHz: $z\approx1$. Hubble: $d=cz/H_0\approx100$ Mpc. Recombination at ~3000 K ($z\approx1100$; "about 1 eV" scale in MC).
- Geometry: $\Omega>1$ closed, positive curvature (angle sum $>180^\circ$).
- BH growth: $L=\eta\dot M_{acc}c^2$, $\dot M_{BH}=(1-\eta)\dot M_{acc}$, $L=L_{Edd}=4\pi GM m_pc/\sigma_T$: $M=M_0e^{t/t_S}$, $t_S=\frac{\eta}{1-\eta}\frac{c\sigma_T}{4\pi Gm_p}=5.0\times10^7$ yr ($\eta=0.1$), doubling $3.5\times10^7$ yr. $10\to10^9M_\odot$ needs $18.4\,t_S=0.92$ Gyr.
