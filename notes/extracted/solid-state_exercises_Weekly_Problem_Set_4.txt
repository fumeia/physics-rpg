Exercise 2: Einstein model for the heat capacity
In the Einstein model, only one frequency is allowed for the lattice oscillations. This cor-
responds to a single optical branch (ω(k) = ωE = const.) of the phonon dispersion. Here
the corresponding contribution to the specific heat of a crystal lattice is to be calculated.
Proceed as follows:

  a) Calculate the thermal energy of a crystal with N primitive cells in this model.

      Hint: Use the Bose-Einstein distribution function for the occupation number of each
      harmonic mode.


  b) Derive the specific heat capacity.


  c) What is the contribution to the heat capacity for high temperatures T ≫ ΘE ?
     What is the contribution to the heat capacity for low temperatures T ≪ ΘE ? Here,
     ΘE = ℏωE /k is the so-called Einstein temperature. Interpret these results.
Exercise 3: Total heat capacity of a diatomic material
One of the assumptions of the Einstein model is that every atom in a solid oscillates
with the same frequency ω0 . However, if the solid contains different types of atoms, it
is unreasonable to assume that the atoms oscillate with the same frequency. Here we
consider a lithium crystal that consists 7.5% 6 Li atoms and 92.5% 7 Li atoms. These are
both stable isotopes and the percentages correspond to their natural abundance. Here,
we will calculate the heat capacity of this crystal by extending the Einstein model to take
into account the different masses of these isotopes. We call the total number of atoms in
the crystal N .


  a) Assume that the harmonic force constant (spring constant) k experienced by each
     atom is the same. In the harmonic approximation, what are the two eigenfrequen-
     cies of the two different isotopes in the lithium crystal?


  b) The lithium crystal is kept at temperature T . Write down the expectation value of
     the energy stored in the vibrations of the 6 Li atoms. Do the same for the 7 Li atoms.
     Write down the expectation value of the total energy stored in the vibrations of all
     the atoms in the lithium crystal.


  c) Compute the heat capacity of the lithium crystal as a function of T . Below what
     temperature are all the atoms mostly in their ground state?
